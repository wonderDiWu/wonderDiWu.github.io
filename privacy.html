Privacy

This website keeps limited server-side visit logs for security and basic traffic analysis. The logs contain the visit time, IP address, requested page, referring site when supplied, approximate country, region and city, and a coarse browser and operating-system label.

No cookies, advertising tracking, persistent browser fingerprinting, or third-party analytics provider are used. URL query strings and detailed referrer paths are not retained. Visitor logs are private, used only for the purposes above, and automatically deleted after 30 days.
